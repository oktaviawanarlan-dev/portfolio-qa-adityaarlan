Review Management

Hotel review-management platform — QA case study covering AI-assisted replies, moderation workflows, analytics, access control, and external review-platform integrations.

01

Overview

An AI-powered review-management platform that centralizes guest reviews from multiple external channels, analyzes review sentiment, generates suggested responses, and enables users to review, edit, approve, or discard replies before publication.

The platform also provides review analytics, property settings, notification and response rules, external channel connections, bulk operations, and event history. My role was quality ownership across the review lifecycle — validating not only AI-assisted functionality, but also workflow consistency, permissions, integrations, and data accuracy.

My Role

Responsible for validating the reliability of the end-to-end review-management workflow — from review collection and AI-generated responses through human moderation, publication, analytics, and audit history.

Collaborated with developers and product stakeholders to identify risks, design test scenarios, investigate defects, validate permissions and integrations, perform regression testing, and verify releases.

Case Study 01

Review Workflow State Consistency

Problem A review action caused inconsistent status or UI behavior between the review list, unanswered count, and related workflow state.
QA Challenge Validate that actions such as edit, approve, delete, discard, and bulk approval update all related views consistently.
Investigation Compared the review state before and after the action across the UI, backend response, filters, counters, and event history.
Outcome Identified the workflow consistency gap and verified the corrected state across the original scenario and related regression cases.
Case Study 02

Permission Enforcement Gap

Problem A restricted account could reach an action or page that should not be available for its permission level or property status.
QA Challenge Verify that restrictions are enforced beyond simple UI visibility.
Investigation Tested different permission levels, account states, direct-page access, and review actions to compare expected versus actual authorization behavior.
Outcome Flagged the access-control gap and verified consistent enforcement across navigation, controls, and protected actions.
